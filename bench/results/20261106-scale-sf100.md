# Scale: SF100 on 32 vCPUs, larger than memory (plan item 32, stage B, 2026-11-06)

Run `scale-sf100-arm64-r2` (job `scale-sf100`, one `n4a-highcpu-32` Spot VM, Neoverse-N3, 32 vCPU / 64 GB, 300 GB data disk, 5,471
VM-seconds, $0.598), code `dd60b6c`. TPC-H SF100 (600 million `lineitem` rows) generated with DuckDB's generator in 10 steps into a database
file on disk (`scripts/gen-tpch.sh`, the chunked path; at SF100 an in-memory generation does not fit). A first attempt the same day was
refused a VM by the project's 96-vCPU quota and cleaned up at $0.000. Raw: `bench/results/raw/20261106-scale-sf100-arm64/`.

## 1. Q1 and Q6 at 8, 16 and 32 threads (the Arrow pipeline spike)

Every block settled, 3 rounds alternated with DuckDB, wall time of the process. **All 18 of our outputs equal DuckDB's digit for digit.**

| query | threads | ours s | DuckDB s | ours / DuckDB | our speed-up over 8 threads | DuckDB speed-up |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| q1 | 8 | 1.668 | 2.135 | 0.78 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| q1 | 16 | 0.883 | 1.195 | 0.74 | 1.89 | 1.79 |
| q1 | 32 | 0.501 | 0.791 | 0.63 | 3.33 | 2.70 |
| q6 | 8 | 0.906 | 1.099 | 0.82 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| q6 | 16 | 0.502 | 0.673 | 0.75 | 1.80 | 1.63 |
| q6 | 32 | 0.305 | 0.530 | 0.58 | 2.97 | 2.07 |

With enough data the pipeline uses the 32 cores: from 8 to 32 threads Q1 gains 3.33x of the ideal 4x (efficiency 0.83), Q6 2.97x. At SF10
the same machine flattened past 16 threads (`bench/results/20261106-scale-sf10.md`): that was the input size, not the scheduler.

## 2. The operators-beyond spike in its spill modes, 32 threads, `ulimit -n 1024`

Spill modes only (`OB_SPILL_ONLY=1`; the in-memory modes do not fit at SF100), a 4 GB budget and 512 grace partitions. **11 of 11
comparisons with DuckDB passed** (multiset equality and row-by-row order), and no spill file was left behind:

| query | rows | seconds | spilled | spill files, most open |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| q13 (grace) | 45 | 18.61 | 2,413 MB | 1,024 files, 512 open |
| join inner / left / right / full / semi / anti (grace) | 1 each | 4.20 to 4.92 | 2,413 MB | 1,024 files, 512 open |
| sort, spill, fan-in 4 | 600,037,902 | 557.84 | 193,592 MB | 710 files, 32 open |
| sort, spill, 1 task, fan-in 2 | 600,037,902 | 973.51 | 126,401 MB | 17 files, 3 open |
| window peers, spill | 150,000,000 | 59.41 | 21,884 MB | 63 files, 24 open |
| window unique, spill | 150,000,000 | 63.52 | 23,481 MB | 75 files, 16 open |

Readings. (1) **Larger than memory works**: the 600-million-row sort spilled 194 GB (three times the machine's RAM) and returned DuckDB's
rows in DuckDB's order; the merge passes kept at most 32 files open. (2) **The grace join is the one that comes near the descriptor
limit**: 512 partitions keep 512 files open at once, half of the 1,024 allowed. More partitions (needed for larger inputs or smaller
budgets, `bench/results/20261106-spill-bench.md`) would hit the limit: slice 7 must bound open partition files (write partitions in
batches or merge them), and its card must test at a descriptor limit below the partition count. (3) DuckDB's own comparison of the sort's
output used 48 GB of the 64 GB and made the VM swap; the reference check, not our engine, is the memory-hungry step at this size.

What this does not show: the in-memory modes and Q18/Q21 at SF100 (skipped: they do not fit); x86-64; more than 32 vCPUs at SF100; the
product engine (these are the spikes; the slice 3 scan was measured at SF10); a real object store.
